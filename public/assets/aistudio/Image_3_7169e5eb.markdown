## Brand & Style

This design system embodies structural integrity, timeless craftsmanship, and urban prestige tailored for an elite South Indian construction and property development brand. Rooted in Chennai's rapidly evolving metropolitan landscape, the aesthetic bridges classical permanence with progressive civil engineering.

The design philosophy combines **Architectural Precision** and **Modern Corporate Rigor**. Visual elements mirror blueprints, structural beams, and refined stone surfaces. Layouts utilize clean horizontal and vertical structural lines, restrained metallic accents, and high-contrast typographic compositions. 

The intended emotional impact is confidence, permanence, and meticulous craftsmanship—reassuring premium homebuyers, commercial investors, and architectural partners.

## Colors

The color palette reflects deep coastal oceanic depths and enduring granite foundations:

- **Primary (`#0B192C`):** Midnight Navy serves as the anchor for headers, primary action elements, dark hero sections, and solid foundations.
- **Secondary (`#1E3E62`):** Steel Azure creates architectural depth for secondary containers, navigation states, and structural dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Golden Amber acts as a controlled accent, signifying warm luxury, vastu-aligned auspiciousness, and quality assurance.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp Architectural Slate provides an airy, daylight-filled canvas that prevents the starkness of pure digital white.

Use Amber sparingly (call-to-actions, status tags, project badges) to preserve its prestige value. Structural wireframes and borders leverage `#E2E8F0` to maintain blueprint discipline.

## Typography

The pairing blends the structured technical geometry of **Space Grotesk** with the balanced warmth and legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Evokes drafting tables, structural blueprints, and modern industrial typography. Always set with tight tracking to accentuate architectural weight.
- **Body & Captions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean readability for property specifications, floor plan details, and legal documentation.
- **Technical & Metric Accents:** Dimensional figures (e.g., `4,200 SQ. FT.`, `RERA APPROVED`) utilize `label-technical` in uppercase with expanded letter-spacing to reinforce engineering precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column grid system that emphasizes alignment, rhythm, and clarity.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, and 3rem (`48px`) outer margins with a maximum container width of `1320px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.5rem gutters, and 2rem outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, and 1.25rem outer margins.

Spacing follows an 8-point base module. Spatial composition prioritizes structural cadence: generous sectional breaks (`space-xl` and above) give developments room to breathe, while spec sheets and technical breakdowns enforce tight, tabular cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp boundary definition over deep drop shadows, inspired by architectural CAD drawings and stone paneling.

- **Surface Layering:** Depth is primarily established through surface gradation (`#FFFFFF` resting atop `#F8FAFC`, enclosed within `#F1F5F9` sections).
- **Architectural Wireframes:** Instead of heavy drop shadows, cards and panels rely on delicate 1px borders (`#E2E8F0` or `#1E3E62` at 15% opacity).
- **Subtle Ambient Elevation:** For floating navigation bars and modals, use minimal elevation: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08)`.
- **Inverted Dark Tiers:** Premium project showcases employ solid `#0B192C` backdrops with hairline amber or slate dividers, commanding authority without decorative excess.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft** geometry (`roundedness: 1`), honoring structural angles and granite-cut finishes rather than playful bubbly radii.

- Standard buttons, form fields, and operational tags feature a clean **0.25rem (4px)** radius.
- Property cards, dialogs, and modular preview containers use **0.5rem (8px)**.
- Full pill treatments are strictly forbidden except for live project status indicators (`Completed`, `Under Construction`), which utilize subtle micro-badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0B192C` with text in `#FFFFFF`, 0.25rem border-radius, `label-lg` styling. Hover transitions to `#1E3E62`.
- **Accent:** Solid `#D97706` with text in `#FFFFFF`. Reserved strictly for lead generation actions (e.g., "Schedule Site Visit", "Download Brochure"). Hover transitions to `#B45309`.
- **Outline:** 1px solid border (`#0B192C`), transparent background, `#0B192C` text. Hover shifts to `#0B192C` fill with white text.

### Cards
- **Property Cards:** Constructed with a white base, thin 1px outline in `#E2E8F0`, and a 0.5rem radius. Images maintain a 16:10 aspect ratio with an inset technical spec overlay (BHK count, carpet area in Space Grotesk). On hover, subtle elevation lifts the card with a 1px border shift to `#1E3E62`.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Crisp rectangular inputs with `#F8FAFC` background and a 1px `#CBD5E1` border.
- Active focus displays a 1px solid `#0B192C` border with an explicit amber outline offset (`2px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags (RERA, Ongoing, Sold Out):** Capitalized `label-technical` micro-chips with 2px corner radius. Backgrounds use 10% tint of the indicator color with full-tone text (e.g., Amber tag: background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1)`, text `#B45309`).

### Floor Plan & Spec Sheets
- Tabular lists featuring alternating hairline dividers (`#F1F5F9`) and two-column data grids. Metric labels in muted slate (`#64748B`), values in bold `#0B192C`.
